Enterprise AI Usage and Governance Policy

1. AI-generated recommendations are advisory in nature and should not replace human judgment.

2. Personally identifiable information (PII) must not be exposed outside authorized enterprise systems.

3. AI systems used in hiring must avoid discriminatory or biased decision-making.

4. All AI-generated hiring recommendations must include explainability and supporting evidence.

5. Sensitive candidate information should only be accessible to authorized personnel.

6. AI models should be periodically reviewed for fairness, accuracy, and compliance.

7. Human oversight is mandatory for all final hiring decisions.

8. Enterprise AI systems must comply with applicable privacy and governance regulations.
