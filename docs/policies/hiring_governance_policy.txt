Enterprise Hiring Governance Policy

1. All hiring decisions must comply with equal opportunity employment principles and applicable labor regulations.

2. Candidate evaluations must be based on documented job requirements, competencies, and role expectations.

3. All shortlisted candidates for Assistant Vice President roles must undergo background verification and employment validation.

4. Candidates with employment gaps exceeding 12 months must provide additional clarification during the interview process.

5. Frequent job changes across multiple organizations within short durations should be reviewed by hiring managers.

6. Interview feedback must be documented and retained for audit and compliance purposes.

7. Hiring decisions for leadership roles should consider stakeholder management, communication ability, and leadership potential in addition to technical expertise.

8. Candidates handling customer or financial data must demonstrate awareness of data privacy, governance, and compliance practices.

9. AI-assisted hiring recommendations should be reviewed by human decision-makers before final selection.

10. Resume data and candidate information must be handled according to enterprise data privacy and confidentiality standards.

11. Diversity and inclusion principles should be considered throughout the hiring process.

12. Final hiring recommendations should include justification aligned to job requirements and governance policies.
